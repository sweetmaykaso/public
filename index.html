Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a currency?
Tap "Add Currency" at the bottom of the list and search for the currency you want to track.

How do I reorder or remove currencies?
Tap "Edit" in the top-right corner, then drag to reorder or tap the delete button to remove.

How do I add a currency to my home screen widget?
Swipe left on any currency in the list, or long-press it, and choose "Add to Widget."

Why do exchange rates look slightly delayed?
Rates are refreshed periodically and cached for offline use, so there may be a short delay compared to live market rates.

How do I remove ads?
Open Settings → Premium → Remove Ads for a one-time purchase that removes ads permanently.
